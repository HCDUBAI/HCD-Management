Player Safety & Participation Rules

Handball is a physical sport and participation in Handball Club Dubai activities is voluntary. Every player is responsible for taking reasonable care of their own health, safety and physical condition.

  1. Complete an appropriate warm-up before participating.
  2. Use appropriate protective equipment where required.
  3. Do not participate if injured, unwell or physically unable to play safely.
  4. Immediately inform the Coach or another responsible team member if you feel unwell or become injured.
  5. Stop participating immediately if an injury occurs and seek appropriate medical attention when necessary.
  6. Follow the Coach's instructions and behave responsibly to help maintain a safe environment for everyone.

I understand that handball involves inherent risks of injury and that I participate voluntarily and accept the normal risks associated with participation.

Rules version HC-DUBAI-SAFETY-V1. You will only be asked again if the rules are materially updated.
